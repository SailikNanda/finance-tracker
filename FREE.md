# 100% Free Stack (Bangla, v2.0)

Finera pure APK-e chole - **zero backend, zero cloud, zero cloud upload**. Phone-e-i sob data thake, phone-e-i API call hoy (Groq, Tavily - free tier).

## Services used

| Service | Kaaj | Free tier | Sign-up laage? | Card laage? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Tavily](https://tavily.com) | Real-time day-to-day currency rates | 1000 searches/month | Hya (email) | Na |
| [Groq Cloud](https://console.groq.com) | AI insights (Llama 3.3 70B) | ~30 req/min, generous daily | Hya (email) | Na |
| Android sideload (Play Store chhara) | App distribution | Free | Na | Na |
